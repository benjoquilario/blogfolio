Sure Win is a mobile review application designed for social work students and aspiring registered social workers who are preparing for the board exam.

The goal of this project is simple: make board exam preparation easier, more affordable, and more effective. Many future social workers spend a lot of money on review centers and printed materials just to prepare for the exam. With Sure Win, I wanted to build a cheaper and more accessible option that helps users study anywhere, memorize answers more easily, and keep a steady review routine using their phone.

The name **Sure Win** also has a personal meaning. It is inspired by **SW**, which can stand for **Social Work**, and the idea behind the app is that with the right review habits and the right tools, it can help guide users toward their win.

## What the app is for

Sure Win is built for social workers preparing for licensure exams who want a more practical way to review without depending entirely on expensive traditional review setups.

Instead of making review feel overwhelming, the app focuses on:

- Easy-to-use board exam review flows
- Repetition and memorization support
- Clear practice modes
- Learning content users can review anytime
- Progress tracking so users know how they are improving
- A community space where reviewees can talk to one another

## Core experience

The app combines reviewer content, quiz modes, and progress tools in one mobile experience.

Users can open review sets, choose a mode that matches their available time, answer board-style questions, and keep track of how much they have already completed. The app is designed to feel simple and guided so users can focus more on learning and less on figuring out how the system works.

The practice flow includes different ways to review, such as:

- Short review sessions
- Practice mode
- Extended mode
- Challenge mode
- Full-item review sessions

This helps users match the app to their own study rhythm, whether they only have a few minutes or want a full-length review session.

## Quiz and memorization flow

One of the most important parts of Sure Win is how it presents questions in a focused and easy-to-follow format.

The quiz interface is designed to help users stay concentrated while answering timed items, reviewing choices, and building confidence through repetition.

Because the app is centered on board exam preparation, the experience is built around helping users:

- Memorize answers more effectively
- Review concepts repeatedly
- Build exam familiarity
- Study in a more affordable way
- Continue reviewing from where they left off

## Key features

- **React Native Mobile App**: Built specifically for mobile-first board exam preparation
- **Expo-Powered Workflow**: Streamlined cross-platform development and deployment
- **Board Exam Reviewer**: Dedicated review experience for social work board exam preparation
- **Multiple Quiz Modes**: Flexible study modes based on item count and time limits
- **Learning Content Review**: Reviewer content that users can revisit while studying
- **Progress Tracking Dashboard**: Monitor review streaks, weekly activity, and learning momentum
- **Saved Progress**: Continue from previous sessions without losing review history
- **Profile System**: Personal account and learner-facing profile experience
- **Community Discussions**: A space where users can talk with other reviewees
- **Subscription Model**: Premium access structure for sustained platform support and content delivery
- **Simple UX**: Designed to be easy to use even for users who just want to start reviewing immediately

## Why this project matters

Sure Win matters because it addresses a real problem for students and reviewees in social work.

Preparing for the board exam can be expensive, stressful, and difficult to maintain consistently. By moving the review process into a mobile app, this project makes studying more flexible and much more accessible. It gives users a practical way to review daily, memorize more effectively, and stay engaged with their preparation without the same financial pressure that often comes with traditional review options.

Sure Win is a learning product built around a very human goal: helping future social workers prepare with confidence. It combines review content, exam practice, progress tracking, and community support into one app that aims to make the path to passing a little clearer and a lot more accessible.
